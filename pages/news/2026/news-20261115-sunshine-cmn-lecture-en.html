Melanocytic Nevus Care & Family Exchange Lecture

Hosted by the Sunshine Social Welfare Foundation · Featuring Dr. C.I. Yen

November 15, 2026 (Sun) 11:30–16:00 Sunshine Foundation (Zhongshan Dist., Taipei) Medical Services

Caring for a child with a melanocytic nevus raises questions that can leave families anxious and unsure: Should it be treated now, or after the child grows up? Should we see a dermatologist or a plastic surgeon? Surgery or laser? On November 15, the Sunshine Social Welfare Foundation will host a lecture and family gathering featuring Dr. C.I. Yen, Director of General Plastic Surgery at our center, together with a Sunshine Foundation counseling psychologist. They will walk families through choosing a treatment, evaluating treatment at different ages, caring for scars after surgery, and helping children build confidence.

Program

Family gathering | 11:30–13:30 (check-in from 11:00)

Share experiences over lunch and become a source of support for one another.

Medical talk | 13:30–16:00 (online-only participants: check in at 13:15)

Speakers: C.I. Yen, MD (Plastic Surgery, Linkou Chang Gung Memorial Hospital) and a counseling psychologist from the Sunshine Social Welfare Foundation.
